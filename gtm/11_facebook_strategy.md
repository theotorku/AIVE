# Facebook Strategy

Role: where HVAC owners actually live. Two motions: (1) organic presence in
trade groups — the highest-trust channel in this vertical; (2) small paid tests
for the free grade. Facebook is a referral/community play, not a broadcast play.

## 1. Groups (primary — weeks 1–12)

Join 8–12 groups: HVAC business-owner groups (e.g. "HVAC Business Owners",
"Service Business Mastery" style communities), metro contractor groups,
ServiceTitan/Jobber user groups, trades-marketing groups.

**Rules of engagement**

- Weeks 1–2: contribute only. Answer marketing questions with substance, zero
  pitching. Group mods ban sellers; earn resident-expert status first.
- Then: value posts, never link-drops. Format that works:
  "I audited 50 HVAC websites to see if ChatGPT can understand them. Average
  grade: C. The 4 most common gaps: … If you want, drop your site below and
  I'll reply with your grade." → grade replies in-thread = public proof loop.
- The in-thread grade offer is the whole engine: each reply is a micro-demo
  seen by hundreds of owners. Move to DM only after the public grade.
- Respect each group's promo rules; use designated promo days where required.

## 2. Founder profile + page

- Personal profile does group work (pages can't post in most groups).
- Business page: 2 posts/week (teardowns, before/afters, benchmark stats —
  recycled from LinkedIn) so paid ads and DM'd prospects see a live page.

## 3. Paid (test from week 6, $500–$750/mo cap)

- **Objective:** lead gen for the free grade. Creative: grade-card image —
  "Your website has an AI visibility grade. Most HVAC companies score a C or
  worse. Get yours free — 60 seconds."
- **Audiences:** (a) retarget site visitors + grade-page abandoners;
  (b) interest stack: small-biz owners × HVAC/contractor interests × target
  metros; (c) lookalike of graded list once >100 emails.
- **Kill/scale rule:** CPL >$25 after $250 spend → kill creative. CPL <$10 →
  double budget. Target: free grade → audit purchase ≥5% (audit CAC <$500,
  fine at $749 price; great if fix attaches).

## 4. Retargeting loop (email × Facebook)

Pre-audited outreach list → upload as custom audience → grade-card ads run
during the 21-day email sequence. Prospects see the grade in inbox AND feed;
multi-touch at ~$0.02/impression.

## KPIs / 90-day targets

- Resident-expert status in ≥3 groups (mods know you; grade threads recur)
- 3 grade-offer threads with 20+ site drops
- 100+ free grades from Facebook (organic + paid)
- ≥5 paid audits attributed · paid CPL <$15 by day 90
