# Market Sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM

Method: bottoms-up (business counts × achievable annual contract value). Blended
ACV assumption: $1,200/yr (mix of one-off audits $399–$749, Audit+Fix $2,500,
monitoring $149–$499/mo). Conservative; monitoring-weighted mix pushes ACV higher.

## TAM — US local-service businesses addressable by AI visibility

US local-service SMBs across the 10 target categories (HVAC, plumbing, roofing,
electrical, remodeling, pest control, garage door, restoration, landscaping,
movers) with a functioning website.

- ~117,000 HVAC contractors alone (2025); plumbing, electrical, roofing each add
  100k+ establishments; combined trades sector counted in the high hundreds of
  thousands of establishments
- Estimate: **~500,000 websited local-service businesses**
- TAM = 500,000 × $1,200 = **~$600M/yr** (US, current categories only)

Expansion TAM (ABI Profiles: agencies, SaaS, professional services,
multi-location; plus ATS trust products) takes this well past $2B — not needed
for the next 24 months.

## SAM — in-scope, reachable, able to pay

Filter TAM to businesses that match ABI v0.1.1 scope and the ICP: real
multi-page site, marketing spend ≥$1k/mo, defined service area, can edit site.
Industry data: HVAC firms budget 5–10% of revenue for marketing; roughly a third
to half of websited trades businesses clear the spend bar.

- ~150,000–200,000 businesses
- SAM = ~175,000 × $1,200 = **~$210M/yr**

## SOM — realistic 12-month obtainable

One founder-led motion, HVAC beachhead in 3–5 metros, then plumbing/roofing
adjacency. Funnel math (targets from strategy §9): 1,500 pre-audited prospects
→ 15% reply → 25% of demos close → ~55–75 paid audits; 30% attach fix; 40% of
fixes convert to monitoring.

- ~60 audits ($749 avg after founding cohort) ≈ $45k
- ~18 Audit+Fix ($2,500) ≈ $45k
- ~7 monitoring retainers ($299 avg × avg 5 months) ≈ $10k
- **SOM year 1 ≈ $100k–$150k revenue**, ~25–30 recurring accounts entering year 2

## Why this market clears the bar

- **Willingness to pay is proven at the price point:** these businesses already
  pay $1k–$5k/mo for SEO/ads; a $399–$749 audit is under one week of ad spend;
  HVAC CPL alone runs $70–$250 — one saved lead pays for half the audit.
- **Tailwind:** AI assistants are an emerging discovery channel; category spend
  (GEO/AEO) is forming at $1.5k–$50k/mo for agencies and $29–$699/mo for tools —
  the SMB one-off entry point is unoccupied (see 03_competitive_landscape.md).
- **COGS ≈ $0.004/audit** → ~99% gross margin; SOM is profitable from customer #1.
