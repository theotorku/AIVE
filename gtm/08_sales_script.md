# Sales Call Script — Audit Review / Close

Companion to `sales/demo_script.md` (demo mechanics) and
`sales/objections.md` (rebuttals). Context: prospect replied to a pre-audit
email; you have their real grade. 25–30 min call. Goal: close rung 1/2 audit,
or (post-audit calls) close the fix.

## 1. Open (2 min)

"Thanks for taking this. Quick agenda: I'll show you exactly what AI systems can
and can't see on {{site}} today, the three things costing you the most, and if
it's useful we can talk about what fixing it looks like. Fair?"

*(Permission-based agenda. No company pitch. The grade is the pitch.)*

## 2. Discovery (5 min) — ask, then shut up

1. "Where do most of your leads come from today?"
2. "What are you spending monthly on ads or SEO, roughly?"
3. "Have you noticed anything change in lead volume or quality this year?"
4. "When a customer mentions they found you — have any said ChatGPT or AI?"
5. "Who makes changes to your website when you need something updated?"

Listen for: CPL pain, SEO distrust, AI curiosity, ability to act (Q5 = fix-deal
qualifier).

## 3. The reveal (8 min) — walk the grade

Share screen: their dashboard result.

"{{business_name}} scores **{{score}} — a {{grade}}**. Here's what that means:
when an AI assistant reads your site, this is what it can confidently say about
you… and here's where it goes blank."

Walk exactly **three findings**, each in this frame:
- **What we found** (show the evidence quote from their own site)
- **Why it matters** ("when someone asks 'HVAC company in {{city}} that does
  emergency repair,' the AI can't confirm you do — so it recommends someone it
  can verify")
- **The fix** (one sentence, plain language)

Then one strength: "To be fair, here's what you're doing right — {{strength}}."
*(Credibility. We're an instrument, not a scare tactic.)*

**Benchmark anchor:** "Across 50+ HVAC sites we've audited, average is a C.
You're {{above/below}} that. The gap between you and an A is specific and
fixable — it's not more content, it's these {{n}} items."

## 4. Temperature check (1 min)

"Before I go further — is this landing as useful? What surprised you?"
*(Their answer tells you close speed and which value prop stuck.)*

## 5. The close (5 min)

**If teaser → audit:**
"What you've seen is the free grade. The full audit gives you all five dimension
scores, every finding with evidence, and a priority fix list your webmaster can
execute — plus a call like this to walk it. That's $749; you're in the founding
ten, so $399. If it doesn't surface at least five actionable findings, it's
free. Want me to run it today?"

**If audit → fix:**
"You could hand this list to your web person — it's written to be executable.
Or we implement it: schema, FAQs, service-area pages, the booking path — then
re-audit in 30 days and show you the number moved. Last engagement went 39 to
53. That's $2,500, and your $ {{audit_price}} audit credits toward it. Which
route fits how you work?"

*(Two-path close. Both paths are wins; the "do it yourself" path converts to
monitoring later.)*

## 6. Objections (redirect to objections.md)

- "Is this just SEO?" → different machine, different rules; fixes help both
- "Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommends us?" → "No — and nobody honestly can.
  What I guarantee is that AI will be able to *read* you, and I prove it with a
  before/after score."
- "I need to think about it" → "Sure. One question — is the hesitation whether
  the problem is real, or whether now's the time?" *(isolate, then handle)*
- "My agency handles this" → "Great — the report gives them a prioritized
  checklist. Want me to include them on the review call?"

## 7. Wrap (2 min)

Confirm next step + date on the calendar before hanging up. Send same-day recap:
grade, 3 findings, the offer, the deadline (founding pricing / calendar link).

## Non-negotiables

- Never promise rankings, mentions, or leads
- Always show evidence from *their* site within the first 10 minutes
- Always leave them with one actionable item even if they don't buy
  (reciprocity → referrals)
