# Recommended First Vertical: HVAC

**Decision: dominate HVAC first.** Expand to plumbing (day 60+), then roofing.

## Why HVAC wins the scorecard

| Criterion | HVAC | Plumbing | Roofing | Remodeling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ABI already calibrated on it (50+ site benchmark) | ✅ | partial | ✗ | ✗ |
| Avg ticket / margin to pay | High ($5k–$15k installs) | Med | High | High |
| Existing marketing spend ($3k–$8k/mo typical) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | varies |
| Recurring demand (seasonal + maintenance plans) | ✅ | ✅ | ✗ (storm-driven) | ✗ |
| Websites substantial enough to audit | ✅ | ✅ | mixed | mixed |
| Tight communities for word-of-mouth (ACCA, FB groups, ServiceTitan) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✗ |
| Business count (~117k US) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

Deciding factor: **the instrument is already validated there.** Every proof
asset we own — the benchmark ("we audited 50+ HVAC sites, average grade C, ~31%
D/F"), the worked fix example (ABI 39→53), the demo — is HVAC-native. Switching
verticals now would restart the credibility clock.

## Domination plan (own the niche, not just sell into it)

1. **Own the number:** publish the *HVAC AI Visibility Benchmark* — the citable
   industry stat. Refresh quarterly. PR it to trade media (ACHR News, HVAC
   podcasts, ServiceTitan/Jobber blogs).
2. **Own the language:** "AI Visibility Grade for HVAC" — every asset speaks
   HVAC (emergency repair, service areas, maintenance plans, financing FAQs).
3. **Own 3–5 metros first** (pick mid-size, competitive-ad markets, e.g.
   Charlotte, Phoenix, Tampa, Columbus): density → referrals inside local
   contractor networks → "everyone's getting graded" effect.
4. **Own the channel:** 2–3 HVAC-focused marketing agencies as white-label
   resellers covers hundreds of contractors.

## Expansion triggers (do not expand before)

- 10 paid HVAC audits + 3 case studies + 1 monitoring client, or
- HVAC pipeline saturated in chosen metros (reply rate <8% on fresh lists)

Then clone the playbook to plumbing — same buyers, same failure modes, ~60% of
outreach assets reusable. Stay inside ABI v0.1.1 local-service scope until ABI
Profiles ship.
