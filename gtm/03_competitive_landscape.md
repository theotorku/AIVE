# Competitive Landscape

Six competitor classes. Nobody combines **site-side measurement + evidence +
fix + verification for local SMBs at a one-off price** — that is the open lane.

## 1. Enterprise AI-visibility platforms

Profound, Scrunch AI, Athena, Ahrefs Brand Radar ($699/mo bundle)

- Track brand presence *inside* AI answers (prompt sampling across 10+ engines)
- Enterprise pricing, brand-marketing buyer, no SMB motion, no fix delivery
- Threat: low near-term. They define the category vocabulary we ride on.

## 2. Self-serve AI-mention monitors

Otterly.AI ($29–39/mo), Peec AI, Knowatoa, SE Ranking AI module (~$103/mo),
Semrush AI toolkit

- Answer "do you appear in AI answers?" — not "*why not* and *what to fix*"
- Prompt-side, not site-side; no evidence trail, no remediation, DIY dashboards
- Threat: medium. Cheap and adjacent; our monitoring rung must beat them on
  "we re-audit and verify fixes," not on dashboard features.

## 3. Free AI-readiness graders

HubSpot AI Search Grader, Ahrefs free checker, WebAloha, AEO Engine, Insites
(local-business AI audit), Tilio

- Single-page technical checks (robots.txt, schema present?, headings) → lead
  magnets for something else
- No crawl, no extraction, no business-fact evidence, no score you can re-test
- Threat: medium-high for perception ("I already got a free AI score"). Counter:
  our teaser *is* the free grader, but backed by a real crawl + evidence; sell
  the difference between a checklist and an instrument (frozen, repeatable ABI).

## 4. AI-SEO / GEO agencies

$1k–$5k/mo SMB retainers; audits from ~$4,500; NoGood, Optimist-listed shops

- Sell labor, not an instrument; slow, expensive, unverifiable for SMBs
- Threat: low direct (different price band) — high *channel value*: they are our
  white-label resellers (secondary ICP).

## 5. Local-SEO incumbents

BrightLocal, Whitespark, Moz Local, Yext — bolting "AI visibility" onto listings

- Own the local-SMB agency relationship; listings/citations focus, not site
  semantics; will commoditize shallow "AI checks"
- Threat: highest long-term. Mitigation: benchmark data moat + fix-verification
  loop + category speed. Partner where possible.

## 6. Status quo

"My SEO guy handles it" / do nothing. The real competitor in every deal.
Counter: the pre-audit grade makes invisibility concrete and personal.
