# Cold Email Sequence — Audit-Led (v2)

Upgrades `sales/outreach.md`: every prospect is **pre-audited before email 1**
(COGS $0.004 — run the whole list). The grade is the hook. 5 touches / 21 days.
Plain-text, from founder, one CTA per email. Stop on any reply.

**List hygiene:** 50–100 HVAC prospects per metro (ICP: 01_icp.md). Verify
emails. Send Tue–Thu, 7–9am local. Track in the KPI sheet (13).

---

{{sender_name}}

## Sequence rules

- **Personalization minimum per email:** grade, one site-specific finding. No
  finding = don't send (re-run the audit or skip the prospect).
- Reply handling: "send it" → send teaser report + book link, not full report
  (full report is the paid product; teaser = grade + top gap + blurred bars).
- Targets: ≥60% open (subject has their grade), **≥15% reply**, ≥50% of repliers
  book. Below 8% reply after 50 sends → rewrite email 1 hook or fix list quality.
- Compliance: CAN-SPAM footer (business address + unsubscribe line), no
  purchased-list spraying; hand-built lists only.
