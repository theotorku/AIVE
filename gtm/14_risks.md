# Biggest Risks

Ranked by (probability × impact). Extends strategy §12.

## 1. Channel-timing risk: "AI visibility" revenue is still emerging

The pitch assumes owners believe AI discovery matters *now*. If prospects agree
it's real but not urgent, deals stall at "interesting."
**Mitigation:** sell clarity + insurance, not rankings; anchor to money they
already spend (one $250 lead ≈ half an audit); founding price to lower the
belief threshold; the competitor-grade email (09 #4) converts curiosity to loss
aversion. **Leading indicator:** reply rate healthy but close rate <15%.

## 2. Free-grader commoditization

HubSpot/Ahrefs-class free "AI graders" make buyers think they've "already
checked." Perception risk, not product risk.
**Mitigation:** our teaser *is* the free grader but crawl-backed; sell the
delta (instrument vs checklist, evidence, re-audit); publish the benchmark to
own credibility. **Indicator:** "I already got a free AI score" appearing in
objections.

## 3. Local-SEO incumbents add "AI visibility" modules

BrightLocal/Yext/Whitespark own the agency relationships and can bundle shallow
AI checks free.
**Mitigation:** speed + vertical depth (HVAC benchmark data they can't
fabricate), fix-verification loop, sign agencies as *our* resellers first.
**Indicator:** reseller prospects say "our platform already includes this."

## 4. Founder bandwidth / single-threaded GTM

One person doing outreach, calls, delivery, fixes, and product. Fix delivery
(rung 3) is the first bottleneck.
**Mitigation:** cap product time at 1 day/wk; fixer playbook keeps delivery
templated; raise fix price before hiring; monitoring is near-zero marginal
work. **Indicator:** audit delivery >5 business days, or outreach pauses.

## 5. Score credibility challenge

A savvy buyer or competitor disputes ABI methodology ("who says 60 is a C?").
One public credibility hit hurts a measurement business disproportionately.
**Mitigation:** frozen spec + deterministic scoring + evidence per finding are
the defense — publish methodology openly; never claim ranking causation; keep
the "5 findings or free" guarantee. **Indicator:** methodology questions on
calls; benchmark pushback.

## 6. Model/ecosystem shift changes what "AI-readable" means

If major assistants shift to relying on proprietary indexes/partnerships over
site crawling, site-side optimization loses some leverage.
**Mitigation:** ABI measures durable fundamentals (clarity, structure,
actionability) that help every channel including SEO; monitoring product turns
ecosystem change into a reason to *stay* subscribed; ATS roadmap hedges toward
trust infrastructure. **Indicator:** platform announcements de-emphasizing
web crawl.

## 7. Open audit endpoint abuse / cost blowout

Public free grade + ungated `/api/runs` = bot-driven crawl costs and queue
saturation.
**Mitigation:** P0 gating (rate limit, captcha, email verification, per-domain
cache) before landing page goes live — see 15, DEPLOYMENT.md §4.

## 8. Sequence/compliance risk in outbound

Cold email at volume without hygiene → spam-foldering, domain damage,
CAN-SPAM exposure.
**Mitigation:** hand-built lists, 20/day cap, separate sending domain, CAN-SPAM
footer, warm-up period, stop-on-reply. **Indicator:** open rate <40%.

## Kill criteria (honesty checkpoint, day 90)

If after 200+ pre-audited prospects and 20+ calls: <5 paid audits, <1 fix, and
reply quality shows "no urgency" as dominant objection → the wedge price/message
is wrong or the channel is early. Pivot options (in order): agency white-label
first motion; monitoring-first bundle at $99/mo; second vertical with stronger
AI-search exposure. Do not spend past month 4 on an unmodified motion.
