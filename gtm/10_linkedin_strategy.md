# LinkedIn Strategy

Role: authority + agency channel. HVAC owners are lighter on LinkedIn than
Facebook — so LinkedIn's primary jobs are (1) category ownership content,
(2) agency/reseller pipeline, (3) warming pre-audited prospects who *are* there.

## Profile setup (founder account — do this before posting)

- Headline: "I grade websites on AI visibility | Agent Business Index |
  Helping local service businesses get found by ChatGPT & Perplexity"
- Featured: free-grade link, benchmark report, 39→53 case study
- Company page for ProPlan: minimal — the founder account does the work.

## Content engine (3 posts/week, 30 min/day)

**Post types (rotate):**

1. **Teardown Tuesday** — anonymized audit of a real HVAC site: screenshot,
   grade, the one gap, the fix. (Ask permission or anonymize; never shame a
   named business.)
2. **Benchmark stats** — "We audited 50+ HVAC websites. Average AI-visibility
   grade: C. Here are the 4 gaps behind almost every D…" Numbers carry these.
3. **Before/after** — fix-engagement lifts (39→53), with what changed.
4. **Category POV** — "AI visibility is not SEO" / "What an AI agent actually
   sees when it reads your website" / myth-busting GEO hype.
5. **Build-in-public** — founding-customer milestones, pricing decisions,
   lessons. Earns agency inbound.

**Format rules:** hook line first, plain text + one image (report screenshot /
grade card), no external link in body (link in first comment), 150–250 words.

## Outbound motions

- **Prospect warm-up:** connect with pre-audited HVAC owners 2–3 days before
  email 1 lands (note: "I run AI-visibility benchmarks for HVAC companies in
  {{metro}} — sending you something this week"). Raises email reply rates.
- **Agency pipeline (weekly, 10 targets):** owners of local-SEO/web shops
  serving trades. Opener: "Do your HVAC clients ask about showing up in
  ChatGPT? We white-label an AI Visibility Audit — $349/audit, you price it
  however you want. Want a sample report with your logo?"
- **DM script for engagers:** anyone who comments on a teardown → "Happy to run
  your site (or a client's) through the grader — no charge. Worth a look?"

## KPIs (see 13_kpi_dashboard.md)

Followers are vanity. Track: free grades from LinkedIn, booked calls,
agency conversations opened, reply-rate lift on warmed prospects.

## 90-day targets

- 36 posts (12 teardowns) · 2 posts >10k impressions
- 300 targeted connections (150 owners / 150 agency)
- 10 agency conversations → 2 signed resellers
- 15% of inbound free grades attributable to LinkedIn
