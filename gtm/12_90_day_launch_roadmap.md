# 90-Day Launch Roadmap

Expands strategy §10 (30/60/90) into weekly execution. One owner (founder).
North star: **first 10 paying customers, first monitoring retainer, HVAC
benchmark published.**

## Phase 0 — Launch readiness (Week 1–2)

- [ ] Ship pre-launch product items P0 list (15_prelaunch_product_improvements.md):
      gate `/api/runs`, teaser-grade output mode, payment link, report branding
- [ ] Landing page v2 live (06) with free-grade capture; analytics events wired
- [ ] Build metro list (3 metros) → 100 HVAC prospects w/ verified emails (01 ICP)
- [ ] Pre-audit all 100 (batch run; ~$0.40 total COGS); QA 10 reports by hand
- [ ] Set up KPI tracker (AIVE_kpi_tracker.xlsx), CRM columns per launch_checklist
- [ ] LinkedIn profile + FB group joins (lurk mode); join 8–12 groups

**Exit criteria:** a stranger can go URL → grade → checkout without you touching
anything; 100 graded prospects ready.

## Phase 1 — First revenue (Week 3–6)

- [ ] Launch email sequence (09) to 100 prospects, 20/day; LinkedIn warm-up 2–3
      days ahead of each batch
- [ ] 3 posts/wk LinkedIn (10); first FB value posts + first grade-offer thread (11)
- [ ] Sales calls per script (08); founding price $399 (first 10 only)
- [ ] Deliver every audit with review call; ask for testimonial + referral at
      each delivery
- [ ] Weekly: update KPI sheet, kill/iterate lowest-performing email

**Targets by day 45:** 15+ replies · 10+ calls · **3–5 paid audits** ·
3 testimonials · 1 Audit+Fix sold

## Phase 2 — Repeatability + expansion assets (Week 7–10)

- [ ] Second 100-prospect batch (add metros 4–5 or deepen 1–3)
- [ ] Deliver first Audit+Fix via fixer playbook; 30-day re-audit → **case study
      with before/after number**
- [ ] Start paid FB tests ($500 cap) + retargeting on outreach list
- [ ] Agency motion: 10 outreaches/wk → target 2 white-label resellers signed
- [ ] Draft the **HVAC AI Visibility Benchmark report** (from accumulated audits)
- [ ] Retire founding price after 10th sale; standard $749

**Targets by day 70:** 10 paid audits cumulative · 3 Audit+Fix · fix case study
live · 1–2 resellers

## Phase 3 — Recurring + own the narrative (Week 11–13)

- [ ] Convert ≥2 fix clients to monitoring ($299/mo) on re-audit proof
- [ ] **Publish benchmark** — landing page + LinkedIn series + pitch to trade
      media/podcasts (ACHR News, HVAC podcasts, ServiceTitan/Jobber blogs)
- [ ] Decide vertical #2 (plumbing) per expansion triggers (04)
- [ ] 90-day retro: funnel conversion review vs targets; reprice/repackage

**Targets by day 90:** **$8–12k cumulative revenue · 2+ monitoring retainers
($600+ MRR) · benchmark published · vertical-2 decision made**

## Operating cadence

- **Daily (90 min):** 20 outreach touches · reply handling · 1 social action
- **Weekly (Fri):** KPI sheet update · pipeline review · 1 experiment decision
  (kill or scale) · progress.md update
- **Rule:** product work is capped at 1 day/wk after Phase 0 — GTM is the job.
  Only exceptions: audit-quality bugs discovered during paid deliveries.

## Contingencies

- Reply rate <8% after 50 sends → rewrite hook (grade in subject), re-verify
  list quality, test phone follow-up on non-repliers
- Calls not closing (<15%) → founding price to $299 for next 5, or add
  "$99 report, credit toward audit" step — do not go free
- Fix capacity bottleneck → productize fix as guided implementation call +
  artifacts pack; raise price before hiring
