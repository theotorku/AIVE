# ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary ICP (launch): Owner-operated HVAC company

**Firmographics**

- US HVAC contractor (residential or resi+light-commercial)
- Revenue $750k–$10M; 5–50 employees
- Defined service area (one metro or county cluster)
- Real multi-page website (10+ pages), able to edit it (in-house or agency/webmaster)
- Already spends on demand gen: Google Ads / LSA, SEO agency, or lead-gen services
  (marketing budget 5–10% of revenue → $3k–$8k+/mo at $500k–$1M revenue)

**The buyer**

- Owner or GM (companies <$3M) or Marketing Manager (>$3M)
- 35–60, pragmatic, burned by SEO promises before; buys proof, not theory
- Active in trade communities (Facebook HVAC groups, ACCA, ServiceTitan ecosystem)

**Pains (in their words)**

- "Leads are getting more expensive every year" (HVAC CPL $70–$250)
- "People are asking ChatGPT instead of Googling — am I showing up?"
- "I pay an SEO guy but I can't tell what I'm getting"
- "My competitor keeps coming up when I ask AI who to call"

**Trigger events (buying signals)**

- Recent website redesign · running Google Ads/LSA · hired/fired an SEO agency
- Owner mentions AI/ChatGPT in posts or podcasts · slow season approaching
- No schema.org markup, weak/missing FAQs, ambiguous service-area pages (pre-audit finds these)

**Success definition for the customer**

- Knows exactly what AI can and cannot see about their business (grade + evidence)
- A fixed, prioritized list of changes; measurable ABI lift after fixes (avg +15 pts)
- Confidence they won't be invisible as AI-driven discovery grows

## Secondary ICP (multiplier): Local-marketing agency / web designer

- Serves 10–200 local-service clients; sells SEO/web/ads retainers
- Needs an "AI visibility" line item to defend retainers and win pitches
- Buys white-label audits in bulk; becomes reseller channel (strategy §7.2)

## Tertiary (defer until ABI Profiles ship)

SaaS, professional services, agencies-as-subjects, multi-location franchises.
ABI v0.1.1 is calibrated for local services only — do not sell outside scope.

## Anti-ICP (disqualify fast)

- No website / one-page placeholder · can't edit their site · no inbound-lead dependence
- Low-ticket services with no margin · wants guaranteed AI rankings
- Franchise locations with corporate-controlled sites (can't act on recommendations)

## Qualification checklist (BANT-lite, 5 questions)

1. Do you depend on inbound leads from your website? (Need)
2. Can you or your webmaster make site changes within 30 days? (Ability to act)
3. Are you currently spending on marketing ≥$1k/mo? (Budget)
4. Is the owner/GM in this conversation or one intro away? (Authority)
5. Did the pre-audit surface ≥2 fixable gaps? (Fit with product)

4–5 yes → pursue. ≤3 → nurture list.
