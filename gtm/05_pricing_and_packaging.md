# Pricing Strategy & Packaging

Refines `sales/offer.md`. Principle: **price on value delivered, not cost**
(COGS ≈ $0.004/audit). Anchor against what the buyer already spends: $3k–$8k/mo
marketing, $70–$250 per lead. Every package is a fraction of one month of ads.

## The ladder (land → expand)

| Rung | Package | Price | What's included | Job it does |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 0 | **Free AI Visibility Grade** | $0 | Letter grade + #1 gap (teaser only, no full report) | Outreach hook / lead capture |
| 1 | **Founding Pilot Audit** (first 10, HVAC only) | **$399** | Full audit, HTML report, 30-min call, listed as founding customer | Logos + testimonials + calibration |
| 2 | **AI Visibility Audit** | **$749** | Full audit, report, 60-min call, written priority fix list | Core repeatable sale |
| 3 | **Audit + Fix** | **$2,500** | Audit + implemented fixes (JSON-LD, FAQs, service/area pages via fixer playbook) + 30-day re-audit proving the lift | ACV driver; creates the case study |
| 4 | **AI Visibility Monitoring** | **$299/mo** (agency tier $499/mo, multi-site) | Monthly re-score, ABI history, change alerts, quarterly human re-audit + fix verification | Recurring revenue; the actual business |

Annual monitoring prepay: 2 months free ($2,990/yr).

## Pricing logic

- **$399 founding** — filters real buyers, low enough for a no-committee yes,
  high enough to command a review call. Expires after 10 sales (scarcity is real).
- **$749 standard** — below the "get a second quote" threshold for a $1M-revenue
  owner; 6–10× under agency audits (~$4,500); pays back with ~5 saved leads.
- **$2,500 fix** — priced against 1 month of a typical SEO retainer, but with a
  verifiable number (re-audit) no retainer gives them.
- **$299/mo monitoring** — deliberately above Otterly ($29–39) and near the
  self-serve category average (~$337/mo): justified by human re-audit + fix
  verification, not a bare dashboard. We move the score, not just watch it.

## Packaging rules

- Sell rungs, never à-la-carte features. Every deliverable maps to an existing
  asset (report, fixer skill, `reuse_extraction` re-scoring) — zero custom work.
- Audit fee **credits toward Audit+Fix** if upgraded within 30 days (attach driver).
- White-label reseller: $349/audit in packs of 5 (agency sells at their price);
  monitoring rev-share 20%.
- Guarantee: "If the audit doesn't surface at least 5 actionable findings,
  full refund." (Benchmark says this never triggers; kills purchase risk.)

## What we never sell (from offer.md — enforce)

Guaranteed AI rankings, guaranteed leads, algorithm access, SEO replacement,
automated rewrites. Sell clarity + verified improvement.

## Price evolution

- After 10 founding: retire $399 permanently (documented price integrity).
- After 25 standard audits: test $949 (value evidence: avg +15 ABI lift).
- Monitoring is the long-term product; by day 180 target >50% of MRR from rung 4.
