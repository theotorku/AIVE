# Qualification

## Best First Customers

Prioritize businesses that:

- sell high-value local services,
- rely on inbound leads,
- have a real website,
- operate in a defined service area,
- already spend money on SEO, ads, or web design,
- have enough margin to pay for visibility work,
- can act on website recommendations.

## Strong Categories

- HVAC
- plumbing
- roofing
- electrical
- remodeling
- pest control
- garage door repair
- water damage restoration
- landscaping
- moving companies

## Good Buying Signals

- recently redesigned website,
- running Google Ads,
- hiring an SEO agency,
- multiple service-area pages,
- active blog,
- online booking or quote form,
- owner talks about ChatGPT or AI,
- poor or missing FAQ content,
- no visible schema.org markup.

## Weak Fits

Avoid early sales time on businesses that:

- have no website,
- have a one-page placeholder with no budget,
- do not depend on inbound leads,
- cannot edit their website,
- sell very low-ticket services,
- want guaranteed AI rankings,
- are outside the local-service scope of ABI v0.1.1.

## Ideal First Outreach List

Build a list of 50 local businesses with:

- category,
- website,
- city,
- contact name,
- email,
- phone,
- notes on visible AI visibility gaps,
- outreach status,
- audit status.

Start with businesses where the audit can reveal obvious, fixable gaps.
