# Productized Offer

## Name

AI Visibility Audit

## One-Liner

Find out whether AI assistants can understand, recommend, and take action on
your business.

## Short Pitch

Your website may look fine to customers, but AI systems read it differently.
The AI Visibility Audit crawls your site, builds a structured business profile,
and scores how clearly AI assistants can understand your services, retrieve
answers, recommend you, and help customers contact or book with you.

## What The Customer Gets

- Overall AI Visibility score
- AI Understanding score
- AI Retrieval score
- AI Recommendation score
- Agent Readiness score
- Semantic Authority score
- Evidence-backed findings
- Prioritized recommendations
- Downloadable HTML report
- Optional review call

## The Business Problem

Customers are starting to ask AI assistants for recommendations:

- "Who is the best plumber near me?"
- "Find an HVAC company that services my area."
- "Which roofing company offers emergency repair?"
- "Book a consultation with a remodeling contractor."

If a website does not make services, locations, trust signals, FAQs, and contact
actions clear to AI systems, the business can be misunderstood, skipped, or
recommended less confidently.

## The Promise

The audit does not promise rankings. It promises clarity.

It shows what AI systems can and cannot confidently understand from the
business website today, then gives the owner a prioritized path to improve.

## Deliverables

### Audit Report

A self-contained report with:

- score and grade,
- dimension breakdown,
- extracted business profile,
- evidence for each finding,
- crawl coverage notes,
- prioritized recommendations.

### Review Call

A 30-minute call covering:

- where the website is clear,
- where AI systems may struggle,
- the highest-impact fixes,
- what to change first.

### Optional Fix Plan

A practical implementation plan for:

- service pages,
- FAQ content,
- schema.org markup,
- booking/contact actions,
- trust signals,
- location/service-area clarity.

## What Not To Sell Yet

Avoid promising:

- guaranteed ChatGPT rankings,
- guaranteed leads,
- direct access to AI model recommendation algorithms,
- full SEO replacement,
- automated website rewrites.

Sell the concrete thing the product can prove: AI readability and actionability
based on the customer's own website.
