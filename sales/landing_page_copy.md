## Problem

Your website was built for customers and search engines.

AI assistants read it differently.

When people ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or future agents for local
business recommendations, those systems need clear, structured information:

- what you do,
- where you serve,
- why you are trustworthy,
- how customers can contact or book,
- whether your content answers common questions.

If that information is missing, unclear, or buried, AI systems may misunderstand
your business or recommend competitors with clearer websites.

## Product

The AI Visibility Audit crawls your website and scores how clearly AI systems
can understand and act on your business.

The audit is powered by the Agent Business Index, a 0-100 score across five
dimensions:

- AI Understanding
- AI Retrieval
- AI Recommendation
- Agent Readiness
- Semantic Authority

## What You Get

- Overall AI Visibility score
- Five dimension scores
- Evidence-backed findings
- Priority fixes
- Crawl coverage notes
- Downloadable report
- Review call

## Why It Matters

AI assistants are becoming a new discovery layer.

Businesses that are easier for AI to understand are better positioned to be
retrieved, explained, recommended, and acted on when customers ask for help.

## Sample Findings

The audit can identify issues like:

- services are listed but not clearly described,
- service areas are ambiguous,
- FAQs are missing or too shallow,
- trust signals are weak,
- booking or quote actions are hard for agents to find,
- schema.org structured data is missing,
- blog content creates noisy or misleading signals.
