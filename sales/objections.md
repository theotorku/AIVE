# Objections

## "Is this just SEO?"

No. SEO is mostly about search engine visibility. The AI Visibility Audit checks
whether AI assistants can understand, retrieve, recommend, and act on the
business from the website content.

There is overlap with SEO, but the audit focuses on machine readability,
structured business facts, answer quality, trust signals, and agent
actionability.

## "Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?"

No. No one can honestly guarantee recommendations inside a specific AI system.

The audit shows whether your website gives AI systems the clear, structured,
trustworthy information they need to understand and recommend your business
with confidence.

## "Why does this matter now?"

Customers are already asking AI assistants for recommendations, comparisons,
and next steps. Even if this channel is early, the fixes are practical:
clearer services, better FAQs, stronger trust signals, structured data, and
easier booking paths.

Those improvements help humans too.

## "Our website already looks good."

The audit is not judging visual design. A website can look good to humans and
still be unclear to AI systems.

The question is whether the site clearly communicates services, locations,
proof, answers, and actions in a way machines can retrieve and trust.

## "Our agency handles this."

Great. The audit can give your agency a prioritized, evidence-backed checklist.

Many agencies are strong at design and SEO but have not yet created a specific
AI visibility workflow.

## "We are not ready to pay monthly."

That is fine. Start with the one-time audit.

After fixes are made, a re-audit or lightweight monitoring plan can track
whether the score improves over time.

## "How accurate is the score?"

ABI v0.1.1 is deterministic and evidence-backed. It uses the crawled website,
structured extraction, confidence scoring, and frozen scoring criteria.

The score is not a claim about traffic. It is a structured measure of AI
readability and actionability.
