# AI Visibility Audit Sales Kit

This folder turns the MVP into a sellable offer.

The product to sell is the **AI Visibility Audit**.

ABI is the score inside the audit. Lead with the business outcome, not the
scoring system.

## Core Positioning

Most websites were built for humans and search engines. AI assistants read them
differently.

The AI Visibility Audit shows whether systems like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
Perplexity, and future autonomous agents can understand, retrieve, recommend,
and act on a business with confidence.

## Best First Market

Start with local service businesses that depend on inbound leads:

- HVAC
- plumbing
- electrical
- roofing
- remodeling
- movers
- home services

These businesses have clear buying triggers:

- they care about visibility,
- they already understand SEO,
- their websites often lack structured AI-readable content,
- missing calls, quotes, and bookings have obvious revenue impact.

## Recommended First Offer

Sell a fixed-scope audit before selling software.

**AI Visibility Audit**

- website crawl and semantic extraction
- ABI score
- five dimension scores
- evidence-backed findings
- prioritized fixes
- downloadable report
- 30-minute review call

Suggested early pricing:

| Package | Price | Use When |
|---|---:|---|
| Pilot Audit | $299-$499 | first 5-10 customers, fast learning |
| Standard Audit | $750-$1,500 | normal SMB sale |
| Audit + Fix Plan | $2,500-$5,000 | includes implementation roadmap |
| Monitoring Retainer | $300-$1,000/mo | recurring score tracking and updates |

Do not lead with a monthly SaaS subscription yet. Sell the audit, learn the
objections, then package recurring monitoring.
