# Launch Checklist

## Before Outreach

- Pick one first category, such as HVAC or plumbing.
- Pick one geography.
- Build a list of 50 prospects.
- Run 3-5 sample audits so the demo has real examples.
- Choose the pilot price.
- Prepare a sample report screenshot or demo URL.
- Decide whether the close is "audit only" or "audit plus fix roadmap."

## Prospect List Fields

Track:

- business name,
- website,
- category,
- city,
- owner/contact,
- email,
- phone,
- visible website gap,
- outreach date,
- response,
- audit sold,
- audit delivered,
- next step.

## First 10-Customer Goal

The goal is not perfect sales automation.

The goal is to learn:

- which prospects understand the problem fastest,
- which findings create urgency,
- what price clears,
- whether buyers want a report, a fix plan, or done-for-you implementation,
- what language customers repeat back.

## First Launch Sequence

1. Pick 50 local service prospects.
2. Send the first cold email.
3. Follow up twice.
4. Offer 5 pilot audits.
5. Deliver each audit with a review call.
6. Ask what was most useful and what was confusing.
7. Convert the clearest demand into the next package.

## Success Criteria

The first launch is successful if it produces:

- 5 real sales conversations,
- 2 paid audits,
- 1 testimonial or case study,
- a clearer offer,
- a stronger list of recurring monitoring needs.
