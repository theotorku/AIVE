# Demo Script

## Goal

Show that the audit turns a vague AI visibility concern into a concrete,
evidence-backed action plan.

## 30-Minute Call Flow

### 1. Set Context

"Customers are starting to use AI assistants as a discovery layer. The question
is whether your website gives those systems enough clear information to
understand, recommend, and help customers take action."

### 2. Ask Discovery Questions

- How do customers usually find you today?
- Which services are most valuable?
- Which cities or service areas matter most?
- Do customers ask the same questions before they call?
- Do you offer online booking, quote requests, or consultation scheduling?
- Have you changed your website recently?

### 3. Show The Overall Score

Start with the grade, but do not dwell on it.

"The score is useful because it shows the shape of the problem. The real value
is the evidence and the fix order."

### 4. Walk Through The Five Dimensions

Keep each dimension tied to business meaning:

- AI Understanding: Can AI tell what you do?
- AI Retrieval: Can AI find useful answers?
- AI Recommendation: Is there enough proof to recommend you?
- Agent Readiness: Can AI help someone contact or book?
- Semantic Authority: Is the site structured and corroborated?

### 5. Show Evidence

Use concrete examples:

- services found,
- services missing or unclear,
- locations/service areas,
- FAQs,
- booking/contact signals,
- trust signals,
- schema.org signals.

### 6. Show Priority Fixes

Keep this to the top 3.

"If we were improving this site, I would start here."

Good first fixes:

- add real FAQ content,
- clarify service-area pages,
- add LocalBusiness schema,
- expose quote/booking actions,
- strengthen service descriptions,
- separate blog examples from actual services.

### 7. Close

"The audit gives you the current state. The next step is deciding whether you
want the fix roadmap or a re-audit after your web team makes changes."

## Demo Rules

- Do not over-explain the algorithm.
- Do not promise AI rankings.
- Do not make the customer feel foolish about their site.
- Do make the gaps feel practical and fixable.
- Always connect findings to customer acquisition.

## Strong Closing Question

"Would you like this as a one-time report, or would it be more useful to pair it
with a fix roadmap your web team can implement?"
