# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Product Name

ProPlan AI Visibility Platform

Core Product:
Agent Business Index (ABI)

---

# Executive Summary

Businesses optimized themselves for:

- Websites
- SEO
- Search Engines

The next era requires businesses to optimize for:

- AI Systems
- Answer Engines
- Autonomous Agents
- Agent-to-Agent Commerce

Most businesses are not machine-readable.

ProPlan AI Visibility Platform measures and improves how effectively AI systems can understand, retrieve, recommend, and interact with a business.

The first product is the Agent Business Index (ABI).

---

# Vision

Become the semantic infrastructure layer that helps businesses become understandable to AI.

Long-term, ProPlan becomes the operating layer between:

Business Data
→ AI Understanding
→ Agent Interaction
→ Revenue Generation

---

# Problem Statement

Business owners increasingly rely on visibility in:

- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Future agent ecosystems

However most websites suffer from:

- Poor semantic structure
- Weak FAQ coverage
- Ambiguous service descriptions
- Inconsistent entity representation
- Limited machine readability

As a result:

- AI cannot accurately understand the business
- AI cannot confidently recommend the business
- Agents cannot effectively interact with the business

---

# Core Thesis

Every business needs a machine-readable representation of itself.

Businesses that are better understood by AI will gain:

- Higher discoverability
- Better recommendation rates
- Better retrieval performance
- Greater participation in future agent ecosystems

# Primary Product

Agent Business Index (ABI)

ABI measures the likelihood that AI systems can:

1. Understand
2. Retrieve
3. Recommend
4. Interact with
5. Reason about

a business.

---

# ABI Framework

## Understanding Score

Measures:

- service clarity
- business clarity
- location clarity
- entity consistency

Weight: 25%

---

## Retrieval Score

Measures:

- content organization
- FAQ availability
- semantic chunk quality
- heading hierarchy

Weight: 25%

---

## Recommendation Score

Measures:

- trust signals
- differentiation
- guarantees
- certifications
- expertise indicators

Weight: 20%

---

## Agent Readiness Score

Measures:

- service accessibility
- contact accessibility
- booking accessibility
- FAQ accessibility

Weight: 15%

---

## Semantic Authority Score

Measures:

- content completeness
- service coverage
- FAQ coverage
- entity coverage

Weight: 15%

---
